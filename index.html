06 / Good questions

A LITTLE
curious?

How do I order?+

Open the menu, tap + Add on what you'd like, then review your order and press Send order on WhatsApp. Your order opens in WhatsApp, ready to send to us. We'll confirm it and tell you when it's ready to pick up.

Do you deliver?+

Orders are for pickup from the café for now. Choose a pickup time when you review your order, and we'll confirm it on WhatsApp.

Is everything plant-based?+

Yes. Everything on our menu is 100% plant-based. For specific allergies, mention them in your order note and ask about ingredients and preparation before ordering.

Where do the prices and nutrition come from?+

They match our café menu. Nutrition is per serving and is a guide. We'll confirm your total on WhatsApp.

How should I keep my cold-pressed juice?+

Keep it refrigerated, give it a good shake, and follow the storage instructions and use-by date on your bottle. Natural separation is normal.
