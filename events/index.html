03 / GOOD QUESTIONS

BEFORE
you come.

Ask us on WhatsApp
Do I need to be vegan to come?+

Not at all. Curious, plant-based or just hungry, everyone is welcome. It's a great first taste of vegan food.

What does the ₹1,499 ticket include?+

Your seat, a full brunch from menu A or B (five dishes including a cold-pressed juice), the No-Bull Burger, a Midnight Brownie, a Fruit Tart, detox water, and the games and entertainment from 9 am to 12 pm. The ticket is ₹1,499 plus 5% GST, so one seat comes to ₹1,574.

Can my friends and I choose different menus?+

Yes. Choose A or B for each seat when you book, for example 2 × A and 1 × B.

How do I pay, and when is my seat confirmed?+

Send your booking on WhatsApp and we'll reply with how to pay. Your seats are held once we confirm them there.

I have an allergy. Can you help?+

Everything is 100% plant-based, so there's no dairy or egg. Some dishes have nuts, soy, gluten or coconut, so please tell us about allergies in your booking note and we'll let you know what's safe.

Where exactly is it?+

The Social Cue, opposite Vegan Valley at South Beach, Kozhikode. Get directions.
